The rule that beats all nine tricks

You can't judge a link by whether the brand name looks right — a perfect sharepoint.com link can still be a trap, and a shortened link tells you nothing. So don't click login or "verify" links in unexpected messages at all. Go to the service yourself with a bookmark or by typing the address, and on your phone, be extra wary of QR codes.
